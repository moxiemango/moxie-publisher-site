Privacy Policy

Moxie Mango Publisher · Effective November 14, 2026

Who we are

Moxie Mango Publisher ("the app") is an internal tool operated by Moxie Mango Design, a small Etsy shop based in Slovakia. Contact: moxie.mango.box@gmail.com.

What the app does

The app publishes Moxie Mango Design's own Etsy product listings to the shop's own Pinterest account (as Pins on its own boards) and to the shop's own Facebook Page. It has a single user, the shop owner. It does not let other people connect their accounts, and it does not read, collect or publish content belonging to anyone else.

Data the app handles

  • Access tokens for the shop owner's Pinterest account and Facebook Page, obtained through the official Pinterest and Meta authorization screens.
  • Account identifiers: the shop owner's Pinterest board IDs and Facebook Page ID.
  • The shop's own public listing data from Etsy: listing titles, images, links and shop sections.
  • Posting records: which listing was published where and when, and the resulting Pin or post ID.

The app does not collect personal data of Pinterest or Facebook users, followers, customers or visitors. It does not read messages, comments, followers or analytics about other people.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the shop's own listings and to avoid publishing the same listing twice. It is never sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising profiles.

Where the data is stored

Tokens and posting records are stored on a private, access-controlled server in the European Union. Tokens are kept in server configuration that is not public and not part of any code repository.

How long the data is kept

Access tokens are kept only while the app is in use and are deleted when access is revoked or the app is retired. Posting records are kept for as long as the app runs, so it does not repost the same listing.

Revoking access and deletion

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest settings (Security and logins / Apps) or Facebook settings (Business Integrations). To have all stored data deleted, see the data deletion instructions or email moxie.mango.box@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
