Moxie Mango Publisher

An internal tool of Moxie Mango Design

Moxie Mango Publisher is a private, single-user tool for the Etsy shop Moxie Mango Design. It publishes the shop's own Etsy product listings as Pins to the shop's own Pinterest boards and as posts on the shop's own Facebook Page, a few per day. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's accounts.
