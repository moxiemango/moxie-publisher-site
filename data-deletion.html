Data Deletion Instructions

Moxie Mango Publisher

Moxie Mango Publisher is a single-user tool that only accesses Moxie Mango Design's own Pinterest account and Facebook Page. It stores access tokens and records of which listings it published.

To remove the app's access

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations → select "Moxie Mango Publisher" → Remove.
  • Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins → Apps → "Moxie Mango Publisher" → Revoke access.

To delete all stored data

Email moxie.mango.box@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion – Moxie Mango Publisher". All stored tokens, account identifiers and posting records will be permanently deleted within 30 days, and you will receive a confirmation by email.
